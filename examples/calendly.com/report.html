Behindsight · Marketing teardown

Calendly

calendly.comCaptured 2026-10-298 pages · 1,257 requests

Calendly sells per-seat meeting software: a free scheduling tier, Standard and Teams plans at $10 and $16 per seat per month billed yearly, and an Enterprise plan starting at $15k a year for 50 seats. Between July and October 2026 it stopped selling itself as "Free Online Appointment Scheduling Software" and relaunched as an AI meeting-management platform, adding a Notetaker, an AI assistant called Callie and built-in payments, with Notetaker and Callie sold through Standard Plus and Teams Plus at $18 and $24 per seat. Organic search is the largest channel this capture can measure — DataForSEO estimates about 276,000 monthly US visits, 69% of top-keyword traffic branded — with a product-led free signup as the main conversion and a "Talk to sales" path for larger teams. Paid advertising looks small: LinkedIn is the only platform where the company's own ads were found, Meta shows no active ads, and no Calendly-named advertiser appears in Google's Ads Transparency Center despite a Google Ads tag on every captured page.

Business model

4 seen · 3 inferred · 1 unknown
